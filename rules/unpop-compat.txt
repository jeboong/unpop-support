! Kernel Video Sharing (KVS) player, kt_player.js (Unpop Privacy).
!
! KVS sites that set the player's anti-adblock option (flashvar protect_block) test two image
! requests about 2 s after the player starts: event_reporting2 (a get_file/ URL) and a bait
! <player dir>/stats.php?... The EasyPrivacy rule `/player/stats.php?` blocks the bait while
! get_file loads, which the player reads as an ad blocker. A few seconds into the video it then
! removes the video, shows its "AdBlocker Detected" notice and exits fullscreen; on iPhone this
! looks like the player reloading.
!
! Evidence (2026-10-29): the KVS 7.x player and archived 6.2.0 builds, run in macOS WebKit with
! an iPhone user agent and the shipped rule files, on a local fixture page carrying a KVS site's
! player options. With the Privacy rules the player is torn down; bisecting them leaves
! `/player/stats.php?` as the only trigger. With these two exceptions compiled into the same list,
! the bait loads, the player does not report an ad blocker, and fullscreen holds. The Ads rules
! never trigger it (the pre-roll VAST is blocked and the video plays). Control: the 5.5.1 build
! has no stats.php bait (its check is an iframe only) and is not affected either way.
!
! Still blocked: the player's own statistics hits wherever the site's event_reporting URL carries
! a parameter after embed=0 (device_type=... or video_id=..., as on every KVS site observed), and
! every other stats.php request. On a site whose event_reporting were a bare stats.php?embed=0,
! the hits without a label would have the bait's shape and pass too: a WebKit url-filter cannot
! tell them apart.
!
! KVS 6.4+ / 7.x bait: stats.php?embed=0&event=<6-15 random letters>,&rnd=<timestamp>
@@/^[^?]*\/player\/stats\.php\?embed=0&event=[A-Za-z]+,&rnd=\d+$/$image
! KVS 6.0-6.2 bait: stats.php?event=Init,  (5.x checks only an iframe; nothing to except)
@@/player/stats.php?event=Init,|$image
