2. What Unpop does

Unpop blocks ads and pop-up ads in Safari on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18.4 or later, using Safari content blockers and a Safari web extension. It does not work outside Safari — for example in the YouTube app, other apps or other browsers. It works only once you turn its extensions on and allow them in Settings, and Safari decides when and how extensions run.

3. Free features and Unpop Pro

Banner and network ad blocking, and blocking of 10 pop-up ads a day, are free. Unpop Pro adds unlimited pop-up ad blocking, the video overlay defence, Unpop Privacy (trackers, cookie notices and app banners) and fewer ad-blocker walls. Features may change as websites and iOS change.
