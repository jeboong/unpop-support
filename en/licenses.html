Open-Source Licenses

Unpop’s blocking rules are built from the filter lists below. Thank you to their authors.

The rules are machine conversions of these lists to Safari’s format: along the way, rules were removed, reordered and trimmed to fit. The converted rules are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Converted rules and sources

  • ads.json — Unpop Ads, Safari content-blocker rules (8.0 MB)
  • privacy.json — Unpop Privacy, Safari content-blocker rules (8.0 MB)
  • popup-hosts.json — the ad-host list built into Unpop Pop-up Blocker (67 KB)
  • unpop-ko.txt — Unpop Korean ad rules, Unpop’s own source list (5 KB)
  • acknowledgements.json — the attribution below, machine-readable
  • README — license, changes, and the version of each list these files were built from

You may share and adapt these files, including commercially, as long as you credit the authors below, say that they were changed, and share your adaptations under the same license (CC BY-SA 4.0). The hostsVN part also carries the MIT notice below.
