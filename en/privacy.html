Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Unpop doesn’t collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no Unpop server. What you browse stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Unpop keeps a few things on your iPhone or iPad so it can work:

  • Your settings, such as whether protection is on and whether the “Ad blocked” note is shown.
  • Counters: how many pop-ups Unpop has blocked, and how many of today’s free blocks are used.
  • The sites you allowed with the note’s Allow button.
  • Whether Unpop Pro is unlocked.

This is stored in the app’s own storage and in the storage Safari gives the extension. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting Unpop deletes it. Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Safari extensions

Unpop Ads and Unpop Privacy are content blockers. They hand Safari a list of blocking rules that ships with the app, and Safari applies the rules itself. Content blockers can’t see the pages you visit.

Unpop Pop-up Blocker is a Safari web extension. To tell a pop-up ad from a pop-up you asked for, it has to run on the pages you visit, which is why Safari asks you to allow it on websites. It makes every decision on your device. It doesn’t record, store or send the addresses or contents of the pages you visit. The only thing it passes to the Unpop app, on the same device, is the number of pop-ups it blocked; the sites you allowed are kept in Safari’s storage on your device.

The blocking rules ship inside the app and are updated with app updates. Unpop doesn’t download filter lists or contact any server while you browse.

Purchases

Unpop Pro is sold through the App Store and Apple processes the payment. We never receive your payment details, name or email address. On your device, the app asks Apple only whether you have Unpop Pro. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

Unpop has no analytics or crash reporting of its own and contains no third-party SDKs. If you chose to share with app developers in iOS (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us in a form that doesn’t identify you. Apple controls this, and you can turn it off at any time.

When you contact us

If you email us or open a GitHub issue, we receive what you send — for example your email address — and use it only to answer you, keeping it only as long as needed for that. GitHub issues are public.

This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Unpop collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, export or delete. To remove everything Unpop stores on your device, delete the app. You can turn its Safari extensions off at any time in Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions. We don’t share personal data with third parties or have anyone process it for us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
