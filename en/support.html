Turn on Unpop in Safari

  1. Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions.
  2. Turn on Unpop Ads and Unpop Pop-up Blocker. With Pro, also turn on Unpop Privacy.
  3. Tap Unpop Pop-up Blocker and set All Websites to Allow. It can only stop pop-ups on sites where it’s allowed.
  4. Open any page in Safari. When the Unpop app shows Protected, you’re done.

Safari warns that the pop-up blocker can read web pages. It needs to, to tell a pop-up ad from a pop-up you asked for — but it decides on your device and sends nothing anywhere (Privacy Policy).

To block pop-ups in Private Browsing too, also turn on Allow in Private Browsing for Unpop Pop-up Blocker.

Blocking stopped after an update

After an iOS or Unpop update, Safari sometimes resets an extension’s website access, and pop-up ads come back.

  1. Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Unpop Pop-up Blocker and set All Websites to Allow again.
  2. Check that Unpop Ads (and, with Pro, Unpop Privacy) are still on.
  3. Check that protection is on in the Unpop app. The app tells you which switch needs flipping.

What’s free, and what Pro adds

Free

  • Banner and network ad blocking (Unpop Ads), always and without limits.
  • Pop-up ad blocking for the first 10 pop-up ads each day. The count resets at midnight, your local time. When they’re used, the next pop-up is still stopped and a note says today’s free blocks are used; after that, pop-ups open as they would without Unpop until the next day. The Unpop home screen shows how many you’ve used today.
  • Pop-ups a page tries to open without your tap are always blocked and never counted.
  • The “Ad blocked” note and per-site Allow.

Unpop Pro

  • Unlimited pop-up ad blocking.
  • Video overlay defence: fake play buttons laid over videos no longer open ads — the first tap plays the video.
  • Unpop Privacy: blocks trackers, cookie notices and app banners.
  • Fewer ad-blocker walls.

Subscription and Lifetime

Unpop Pro comes as a monthly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or as a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store, in your local currency.

To cancel the subscription, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Unpop and tap Cancel Subscription (Unpop’s Settings › Manage Subscription opens the same page). Cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial or the current month ends to avoid the next charge. Pro stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Lifetime is not a subscription: it never renews and is never charged again.

Restore purchases

In Unpop, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Pro comes back on any iPhone or iPad signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can’t see your payment details, so we can’t refund purchases ourselves.

Allow a site, or fix a page that looks wrong

When Unpop stops a pop-up, a small Ad blocked note appears at the bottom of the page with an Allow button. If the pop-up was something you wanted, tap Allow: from then on, that site can open pop-ups.

To block pop-ups again on every site you allowed, open Unpop and tap Settings › Clear Allowed Sites. To hide the note, turn off Settings › Show “Ad blocked” in Safari.

If part of a page is missing or doesn’t work, Unpop Ads or Unpop Privacy may be hiding it. In Safari, open the page menu in the address bar and tap Turn Off Content Blockers to load that page without them. Please tell us the site so we can fix it.

Does Unpop block ads in other apps or the YouTube app?

No. Unpop works only in Safari. iOS doesn’t let Safari extensions work inside other apps — such as the YouTube app, games or social media apps — or in other browsers.

Can Unpop see the pages I visit?

Unpop Ads and Unpop Privacy are content blockers: Safari applies their rules itself and never shows them your pages. Unpop Pop-up Blocker looks at the page you tap on — on your device — to tell ads from pop-ups you asked for. It doesn’t keep your browsing or send it anywhere. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
