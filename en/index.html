Pop-up ad blocker for Safari on iPhone and iPad

Tap anything — no pop-up ad opens.

Some pages turn every tap — on a link, a button, a video — into a new tab full of ads. Unpop stops that in Safari, and blocks banner ads along the way.

What Unpop does

  • Unpop Pop-up Blocker

    Stops the new-window, new-tab and pop-under ads that open when you tap a page. Real links, sign-ins and payments keep working. A small “Ad blocked” note shows what was stopped, with an Allow button for the site.

    • Free: 10 a day
    • Pro: unlimited

  • Unpop Ads

    Blocks banner and network ads with filter lists built into the app.

    • Free

  • Unpop Privacy

    Blocks trackers, cookie notices and app banners.

    • Pro

Unpop Pro

Unlimited pop-up blocking, and fake play buttons over videos no longer open ads — the first tap plays the video. Pro also turns on Unpop Privacy and gets you fewer ad-blocker walls. Choose a monthly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

Safari only

Unpop works in Safari on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18.4 or later. It does not block ads inside other apps — such as the YouTube app, games or social media apps — or in other browsers: iOS doesn’t let Safari extensions reach them.

Nothing leaves your device

No account, no analytics, no Unpop server. Safari applies Unpop’s rules on your device, and the pop-up blocker makes its decisions on your device. Privacy Policy
